Table design proposal · macOS pinball project

Nova Rush

A single-playfield, space-themed table in the classic 20.25″ × 42″ Williams proportions: two-flipper Italian bottom, a three-bumper nest, one full-length return ramp, two target banks, an orbit with spinner, and a mode/lock saucer. Every feature below maps to a physics body and a rule in code.

Feature key

  1. Shooter lane & skill shot — plunge strength picks a lit NOVA lane; soft plunge scores the skill shot.
  2. NOVA rollover lanes — four lanes; complete N-O-V-A to advance the bonus multiplier. Lit lanes rotate with flipper buttons (lane change).
  3. Pop bumper nest — three bumpers; each hit kicks the ball outward, scores, and builds the Nova value.
  4. Nebula saucer — kickout hole. Starts modes when lit; locks balls for multiball.
  5. Left orbit & spinner — full loop to the top lanes; the spinner rips points on the way through.
  6. Comet ramp — the marquee shot: right-side entrance climbing over the bumper nest and returning to the left inlane. Repeatable combo shot; jackpots live here.
  7. Drop-target bank — three drop targets; clearing the bank lights the saucer for mode start.
  8. Star standups — three standup targets; each hit charges the jackpot value.
  9. Slingshots — kicker triangles above the flippers that fling the ball sideways — the classic source of chaos.
  10. Inlanes / outlanes — inlanes feed the flippers and light ramp combos; outlanes drain (nudging matters here).
  11. Flippers — standard two-flipper Italian bottom; full stroke in ~120 ms, momentum transfers to the ball.
  12. Drain & ball saver — center drain; a timed ball saver returns quick losses to play.
Ruleset

How a game plays

Launch

Skill shot

Soft plunge into the lit NOVA lane: 500K and instant bonus ×2.

Build

Nova modes

Clear the drop bank to light the saucer. Three timed modes: Asteroid Storm, Solar Flare, Gravity Well.

Cash in

Multiball

Lock two balls in the saucer; 3-ball multiball with 250K ramp jackpots, super jackpot 1M.

Multiply

NOVA bonus

Lane completions raise the end-of-ball bonus multiplier up to ×8.

Finale

Supernova

Finish all three modes to reach the wizard mode: everything lit, everything scores.

Physics spec (from real machines)

Playfield20.25 × 42 in — 1 : 2.07 aspect, rendered ~460 × 954 pt
Pitch6.5° — effective downfield gravity g·sin 6.5° ≈ 1.11 m/s²
BallØ 1-1/16 in (27 mm) steel, ~80 g, low rolling friction on slick playfield
Flipper~120 ms full stroke, ~48° sweep; kinematic body, momentum transferred at contact
Integration60 Hz frame, physics substepped; continuous collision detection on the ball (anti-tunneling)
KickersSlingshots / bumpers modeled as impulse emitters triggered by contact, not springs
Assessment

Feasibility & build order

Verdict: realistic. Swift + SpriteKit gives us a Metal-rendered 2D scene graph with a built-in Box2D-style physics engine, particle emitters, and positional audio — every feature on this table maps directly to a SpriteKit primitive, and the toolchain on this machine builds a signed macOS .app today. The one genuinely hard part is flipper feel; that is tuning time, not risk.
  1. Rolling steel — table geometry, gravity, ball, flippers, plunger; playable skeleton.
  2. Kickers & score — bumpers, slingshots, targets, spinner, scoring, HUD.
  3. The Comet ramp — elevated-layer trick, orbit, lanes, saucer, modes and multiball.
  4. Light & sound — particles, lamp shows, DMD-style display, full sound package, nudge/tilt.
  5. Ship it — attract mode, high scores, app icon, .app bundle.
